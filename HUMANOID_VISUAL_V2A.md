# Humanoid Visual V2A

This update changes the rendered humanoid aesthetic only. Simulation coordinates, SFM radii, collision behaviour, acquisition distances, catch distances, Dasher rules, Purple priority logic, queue behaviour, timings and metrics are unchanged.

## Visual changes

- More natural body proportions with a visible neck, shoulder line, tapered torso and pelvis.
- Separate thighs, lower legs and shoes.
- Separate upper sleeves, forearms and hands.
- Three-part chamfered head instead of one rectangular head block.
- Semantic status/role colour is concentrated on the upper body.
- Trousers and shoes use professional neutral navy/charcoal tones.
- Head/hands use a neutral skin-like palette.
- Purple operators receive a restrained light uniform trim.
- Dasher hover/click bounds were expanded only to follow the larger visual silhouette; world-space physics are unchanged.

## Deferred to Step 2B

Walking/running gait articulation, forward lean, velocity-facing and body bob are intentionally not part of this update.
