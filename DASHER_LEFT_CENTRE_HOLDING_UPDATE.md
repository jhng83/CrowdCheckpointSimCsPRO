# Dasher Left-Centre Holding Update

The secured Dasher group still consists of exactly two Purple operators plus one Dasher.
After the existing 1-second secure stop, the group now walks at the existing group escort speed to the **left-centre of the main hall** instead of the previous left-corner holding point.

For a 10 m-high hall, the target is approximately `(x=1.0 m, y=5.0 m)`. The x-coordinate retains a safe inset from the left wall and the y-coordinate is always derived from half of the configured hall height.

The existing 10-second holding duration, Dasher removal, Purple return-to-post behaviour, right-of-way, SFM, Yellow handling, acquisition rules, exact-two-catcher guardrail, and Humanoid V2B rendering/animation remain unchanged.
