# Purple absolute Dasher priority and general right-of-way

This update keeps the existing Dasher acquisition radius and exact-two-catcher guardrails, but makes Dasher response the highest-priority Purple task.

- Any Purple operator in normal Yellow work may switch to the Dasher when the Dasher is inside that operator's configured acquisition radius. This includes `Pursuing`, cooperative-stop/catch `Stopping`, `Escorting`, `Verifying`, and `Returning`.
- An operator already assigned to the current Dasher remains on that Dasher task. Once the two-catcher apprehension team is locked, the existing exact-two-catcher rule still releases all extra responders back to their own posts.
- If Yellow verification had already started, the Yellow remains stopped and autonomously completes only the remaining verification time, then becomes Cleared and resumes SFM movement.
- If a Yellow was only being pursued, stopped, caught, or escorted and verification had not started, the Yellow is released back into ordinary Undetected SFM movement and remains eligible for later acquisition.
- Every Purple operator has right-of-way whenever it is outside its own post radius and not idle at its station. This applies to normal Yellow pursuit/escort, Dasher pursuit/control/escort, and return-to-post movement. Ordinary passengers yield using the existing wider priority corridor and body-separation rules.

All other queue, SFM, outcome, acquisition-radius, catch-distance, fatigue, manual-clearance, Dasher two-catcher, holding-area, and timing rules are retained.
