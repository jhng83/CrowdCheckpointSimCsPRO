# Dasher First-Catch Hold Update

This update changes only the optional Dasher incident workflow.

- Dasher running speed: 2.5 m/s.
- Purple Dasher pursuit speed: 3.5 m/s.
- Multiple free Purple operators inside the configured acquisition radius may pursue the same Dasher simultaneously.
- First physical catch at <= 0.5 m: the Dasher and first catcher stop immediately and remain stationary.
- While the pair is stopped, other eligible Purple responders may continue pursuing the same Dasher.
- Second physical catch: apprehension threshold is reached. Any additional responders already within catch distance are included in the catcher group.
- The secured group keeps the existing 1.0 s stop, then escorts the Dasher to the left-centre holding point at 1.10 m/s.
- The Dasher remains at the holding point for 10 s, disappears, and involved Purple operators return to their respective posts.
- A Dasher can escape only while still in the active Dashing state. Once the first physical catch occurs, the incident is secured and no longer disappears through the exit.

Normal Undetected pursuit, verification, fatigue, operator health, queue movement, SFM and acquisition configuration are unchanged.
